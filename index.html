Questions

Know what you're getting.

Does DreamGoals Pro need an account?

No. The current edition runs locally in your browser and does not require a SmartTools Studio account.

Where is my data saved?

In the browser storage on the device where you use it. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices. Cloud sync is not included.

Does it include live AI?

No. This first edition does not connect to a live AI service. Goal-planning calculations are rule-based.

Can I use it on my phone?

The interface is responsive. Compatibility should still be checked on your own device and browser before relying on it.

What is the launch price?

The proposed one-time launch price is Rs. 499 in Pakistan and $7.99 internationally. Paid ordering is not open yet because checkout and automatic delivery are still being prepared.
